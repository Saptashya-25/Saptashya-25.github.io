Saptashya Ghosh

I am a Research Associate in the Information Systems area at the Indian School of Business. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Economics from the University of Calcutta, Kolkata, and a Master's in Economics from Shiv Nadar University, Delhi NCR.

My research sits at the intersection of the two fields, using an economist's toolkit to study digital platforms and their welfare effects in digital markets. I am particularly interested in how technological innovation reshapes these platforms; what it displaces, what it leaves untouched; and in how platform design, market structure, and policy shape outcomes for users and firms.
