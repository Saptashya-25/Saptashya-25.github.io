Saptashya Ghosh

Contact saptashya_ghosh@isb.edu

I am a Research Associate at the Indian School of Business. I hold a Master’s degree in Economics from Shiv Nadar University, Delhi-NCR and a Bachelor’s degree in Economics from the University of Calcutta, Kolkata.

I am interested in studying consumer choices and firm decisions in digital markets, particularly how consumers allocate their time across competing products and how these choices evolve with technological change. On the firm side, I am interested in understanding how digital platforms compete for consumer attention and adapt their strategies as preferences and patterns of technology use evolve. I use econometrics and structural modelling approaches to study consumers and firms in digital markets, with a particular focus on the implications for market outcomes and social welfare.
