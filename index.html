Saptashya Ghosh

I am a Research Associate at the Indian School of Business. I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Economics from the University of Calcutta, Kolkata, and a Master’s degree in Economics from Shiv Nadar University, Delhi-NCR.

I am interested in working on consumer and firm's behaviour in digital markets and its impact on welfare. I use econometrics and structural modelling as a toolkit to study digital platforms and their welfare effects in digital markets. I am particularly interested in how consumers allocate their time across competing products and how these choices are influenced by technological change. I am equally interested in the firms’ side, in understanding how platforms compete for attention and market their products as consumer preferences evolve with technology.
